About The Course Compass

Helping students explore courses, understand admission pathways, discover entrance exams and make informed education choices.

Our Purpose

Choosing a course after school can be confusing. Students often have questions about eligibility, entrance exams, admission processes, career options and what they can do after completing a course.

The Course Compass is designed to bring useful education and admission information together in one easy-to-understand place.

What We Cover

We provide information across a wide range of study areas and help students explore different academic pathways.

🎓 Engineering & IT

Engineering, computer science, IT and emerging technology fields.

🩺 Medical & Allied Health

Medical and allied health pathways and related specializations.

💊 Nursing & Pharmacy

Nursing, pharmacy and related healthcare education options.

📊 Management & Commerce

Business, management, commerce and related career pathways.

⚖️ Law

Law courses, admission pathways and entrance examination information.

🔬 Science

Science courses and options for further studies and careers.

🎨 Design & Architecture

Design, architecture and creative academic pathways.

🌱 Agriculture

Agriculture and related academic and career options.

Our Approach

We aim to keep information simple, organized and useful for students and families. Our goal is to help students understand their options rather than make decisions for them.

We are also developing free practice resources so students can prepare for entrance examinations and strengthen their fundamentals.

Free Admission Guidance

Students can submit an enquiry through our website for admission-related guidance. The Course Compass provides free admission guidance through the enquiry system.

Friendly reminder: The Course Compass does not ask students to pay cash for admission guidance. Please do not pay cash to anyone claiming to represent The Course Compass. For clarification, contact thecoursecompass@outlook.com.
