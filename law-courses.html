Law Courses

Explore undergraduate and postgraduate Law pathways in India, including integrated 5-year law programmes, 3-year LL.B, LL.M and related legal career options.

Choose your pathway: Students can enter integrated law programmes after Class 12 or pursue a 3-year LL.B after completing a bachelor's degree. Postgraduate specialisation is available through LL.M and other advanced study.

Law Education: An Overview

A career in law can lead to opportunities in litigation, corporate legal work, legal compliance, judiciary-related pathways, public services, policy, research, academics and other legal functions.

⚖️ Legal Practice

Litigation, legal advisory, drafting, negotiation and representation form important areas of legal practice.

🏢 Corporate Law

Lawyers can work with companies in contracts, compliance, corporate transactions, employment and legal risk.

🏛️ Public & Policy

Law graduates can explore public services, policy, governance, legal research and related opportunities.

Undergraduate Law Courses

Students completing Class 12 can explore integrated law programmes that combine a bachelor's degree with LL.B.

BA LL.B

An integrated undergraduate programme combining arts and humanities subjects with legal education.

  • Usually a 5-year integrated pathway
  • Entry after Class 12, subject to current eligibility
  • Combines subjects such as political science, sociology or economics with law
  • Career: Litigation, corporate law, policy, legal services and more

BBA LL.B

An integrated law programme combining business and management education with legal studies.

  • Usually a 5-year integrated pathway
  • Entry after Class 12, subject to current eligibility
  • Useful for students interested in business and law
  • Career: Corporate law, contracts, compliance and legal advisory

B.Com LL.B

An integrated programme combining commerce education with professional legal studies.

  • Usually a 5-year integrated pathway
  • Suitable for students interested in commerce and law
  • Accounting, business and legal subjects may be combined
  • Career: Corporate, commercial, tax and legal roles

BSW LL.B

An integrated programme combining social work education with legal studies at institutions that offer the pathway.

  • Integrated undergraduate law pathway
  • Availability varies by institution
  • Can suit students interested in social justice and law
  • Career options depend on qualification and professional pathway

3-Year LL.B

LL.B After Graduation

The 3-year LL.B is designed for graduates who already hold a bachelor's degree and want to pursue professional legal education.

  • Generally entered after a bachelor's degree
  • Typically a 3-year law programme
  • Eligibility varies by institution and applicable rules
  • Career: Legal practice and other law-related pathways

Who Should Consider It?

It can be suitable for graduates from commerce, arts, science, management and other disciplines who decide to enter law.

  • Useful for career changers
  • Allows graduates to enter legal education
  • Prior degree discipline may vary by institution
  • Current eligibility should always be checked

Postgraduate & Advanced Law Courses

LL.M

Master of Laws provides postgraduate-level legal education and opportunities for specialisation.

  • Postgraduate law programme
  • Requires the relevant law qualification as per current rules
  • Specialisations vary by university
  • Career: Specialised legal practice, academics, research and policy

Legal Research

Research-oriented legal education can lead toward academic, policy and specialised research careers.

  • Suitable for students interested in research
  • Can involve constitutional, commercial, criminal or other legal fields
  • Advanced qualifications may be useful
  • Career: Research, policy and academics

Ph.D. in Law

Doctoral-level legal research for candidates interested in advanced scholarship and academic careers.

  • Doctoral research pathway
  • Eligibility depends on university requirements
  • Research proposal and academic requirements may apply
  • Career: Research, teaching and legal scholarship

Diploma & Certificate Programmes

Institutions may offer specialised short-term programmes in areas such as cyber law, taxation, intellectual property, corporate law and other legal subjects.

  • Duration varies
  • Useful for additional knowledge or specialisation
  • Recognition depends on the issuing institution
  • Check course credibility before enrolling

Law Entrance Exams & Admission Pathways

CLAT

A major national law entrance examination used by participating National Law Universities and other institutions according to their admission policies.

AILET

A national-level entrance examination associated with admission to law programmes at the concerned institution.

State / University Entrance

Various states and universities may conduct their own entrance examinations or admission processes for law programmes.

Merit / Institutional Admission

Some colleges may use qualifying examination marks or institution-specific admission criteria.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I study law after Class 12?

Yes. Students can explore integrated 5-year law programmes after Class 12, subject to the current eligibility criteria.

Can I study LL.B after graduation?

Yes. Eligible graduates can explore 3-year LL.B programmes where offered.

Is CLAT required for every law college?

No. Admission requirements vary by institution. Some use CLAT, while others may have separate entrance or merit-based processes.

Can Commerce students study law?

Yes. Commerce students can explore integrated programmes such as B.Com LL.B or BBA LL.B, as well as other eligible law pathways.

What is better: 5-year LL.B or 3-year LL.B?

The choice depends on your entry point. The 5-year integrated route is designed for students after Class 12, while the 3-year LL.B is for eligible graduates.

Can a law graduate work in corporate companies?

Yes. Corporate legal teams hire eligible legal professionals for contracts, compliance, transactions, advisory and related functions.
