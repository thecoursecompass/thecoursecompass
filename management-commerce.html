CA, CS, CMA and other professional pathways can provide specialised careers in accounting, finance and corporate work.

Management Courses

Management programmes can prepare students for careers across business functions, industries and entrepreneurial ventures.

BBA

Bachelor of Business Administration introduces students to management, business operations, marketing, finance and organisational functions.

  • Undergraduate management programme
  • Suitable for students interested in business and management
  • Specialisations vary by college
  • Career: Management, sales, marketing, HR, operations and more

BMS

Bachelor of Management Studies focuses on management concepts, business decision-making and organisational practices.

  • Undergraduate management pathway
  • Business and management-oriented curriculum
  • Admission rules vary by institution
  • Career: Business, management, consulting and corporate roles

BBM

Bachelor of Business Management provides undergraduate education in business administration and management.

  • Undergraduate programme
  • Management and business fundamentals
  • Specialisations vary by institution
  • Career: Business, sales, marketing, HR and operations

MBA

Master of Business Administration is a postgraduate management programme with opportunities for specialisation.

  • Postgraduate management degree
  • Open to eligible graduates from recognised disciplines
  • Specialisations include Finance, HR, Marketing, Operations and more
  • Career: Management, consulting, corporate and leadership roles

PGDM

Post Graduate Diploma in Management is a postgraduate management pathway offered by eligible institutions.

  • Postgraduate management programme
  • Curriculum varies by institute
  • May offer industry-oriented specialisations
  • Career: Management, consulting, marketing, finance and operations

MMS

Master of Management Studies is a postgraduate management programme offered by selected institutions.

  • Postgraduate management programme
  • Eligibility varies by institution
  • Specialisations depend on the programme
  • Career: Corporate management and business functions

Commerce Courses

Commerce education develops knowledge in accounting, finance, economics, taxation, business and related areas.

B.Com

Bachelor of Commerce is a popular undergraduate pathway covering accounting, finance, business and commerce-related subjects.

  • Undergraduate commerce programme
  • Suitable for students interested in accounting and business
  • Specialisations vary by university
  • Career: Accounts, finance, banking, taxation and business roles

B.Com Honours

An undergraduate commerce pathway with deeper study in selected commerce and business disciplines.

  • Undergraduate programme
  • Curriculum varies by institution
  • Can support professional and postgraduate pathways
  • Career: Finance, accounting, banking and corporate roles

M.Com

Master of Commerce provides postgraduate study in commerce, accounting, finance, economics and related areas.

  • Postgraduate commerce programme
  • Relevant undergraduate qualification generally required
  • Useful for academic and specialised commerce pathways
  • Career: Finance, accounting, teaching, research and corporate roles

Economics

Economics programmes explore markets, policy, business, quantitative analysis and economic decision-making.

  • UG and PG pathways available
  • Strong analytical and quantitative skills are useful
  • Can lead to finance, research and policy-related careers
  • Higher studies can broaden opportunities

Professional Commerce Qualifications

CA – Chartered Accountancy

A professional pathway focused on accounting, auditing, taxation, financial reporting and related areas.

  • Professional qualification pathway
  • Structured stages and examinations apply
  • Strong commitment and consistent study are important
  • Career: Audit, taxation, finance, accounting and advisory

CS – Company Secretary

A professional pathway focused on corporate law, governance, compliance and company-related matters.

  • Professional qualification pathway
  • Corporate law and governance focused
  • Examination and training requirements apply
  • Career: Corporate compliance, governance and secretarial roles

CMA – Cost & Management Accountancy

A professional pathway focused on costing, management accounting, financial planning and business decision support.

  • Professional qualification pathway
  • Cost and management accounting focused
  • Structured examinations and requirements apply
  • Career: Costing, finance, management accounting and analysis

Company & Finance Certifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better: BBA or B.Com?

BBA generally has a stronger management and business focus, while B.Com focuses more on commerce, accounting and finance. The better choice depends on your interests and career goals.

Can a Commerce student do an MBA?

Yes, eligible graduates can pursue MBA programmes. Exact eligibility and entrance requirements depend on the institution.

Can Science or Arts students study management?

Many management programmes accept students from different academic backgrounds, subject to the specific institution's rules.

Which is better for accounting: B.Com or CA?

They are different pathways. B.Com is an undergraduate degree, while CA is a professional qualification with its own examination and training structure. They can also complement each other.

Is an MBA necessary for a management career?

Not always. Management careers can begin after undergraduate study, while an MBA can provide advanced education and access to additional career pathways.

Can I work in finance after B.Com?

Yes. B.Com graduates can explore entry-level finance, accounting, banking and business roles, with additional qualifications helping for specialised positions.
