Agriculture Courses

Explore Agriculture and related courses in India, including B.Sc Agriculture, Agricultural Engineering, Horticulture, Forestry, Dairy Technology, Fisheries, Food Technology and more.

Explore your pathway: Agriculture is a broad field covering crop science, soil, horticulture, agricultural technology, food systems, livestock, fisheries, forestry, agribusiness and sustainable resource management.

Agriculture Education: An Overview

Agriculture-related education combines biological science, technology, economics and practical field knowledge. Students can choose between core agricultural science, engineering, food and allied sectors, depending on their interests.

🌾 Crop & Soil Science

Crop production, agronomy, soil science, plant protection and agricultural resource management.

🚜 Agricultural Technology

Farm machinery, irrigation, agricultural engineering, precision agriculture and technology applications.

🥛 Food & Allied Fields

Food technology, dairy, fisheries, horticulture, forestry and other agriculture-linked disciplines.

Popular Agriculture & Allied Courses

🌾 B.Sc Agriculture

A broad undergraduate programme covering crop production, soil, plant science, agricultural economics, extension and related agricultural subjects.

  • Usually a 4-year undergraduate pathway
  • Subject and minimum-mark requirements vary
  • Practical and field-based learning is important
  • Career: Agriculture, agribusiness, research and related sectors

🚜 B.Tech Agricultural Engineering

Combines engineering principles with agriculture, including machinery, irrigation, farm structures and processing.

  • Engineering-oriented agriculture programme
  • Mathematics and science foundations are useful
  • Technology and practical applications are important
  • Career: Agricultural technology, machinery, irrigation and industry

🌱 B.Sc Horticulture

Focuses on fruits, vegetables, flowers, plantation crops, nursery management and horticultural production.

  • Plant-science focused programme
  • Field and practical learning may be included
  • Can lead to postgraduate specialisation
  • Career: Horticulture, nurseries, production and research

🌲 B.Sc Forestry

Covers forests, biodiversity, conservation, forest resources, ecology and sustainable management.

  • Environmental and biological science pathway
  • Fieldwork can be an important component
  • Higher studies can provide specialisation
  • Career: Forestry, conservation, research and environmental fields

🐟 Fisheries Science

Studies aquatic resources, fisheries, aquaculture, fish biology, production and resource management.

  • Specialised agriculture-allied pathway
  • Biology and practical science are important
  • Aquaculture is a related application area
  • Career: Fisheries, aquaculture, research and related sectors

🥛 Dairy Technology

Focuses on milk processing, dairy products, quality, technology and dairy industry operations.

  • Food and processing-oriented programme
  • Science and technology are important
  • Quality and processing skills are useful
  • Career: Dairy industry, quality, processing and research

🍎 Food Technology

Covers food processing, preservation, food chemistry, microbiology, quality and food production.

  • Applied science and technology pathway
  • Laboratory and processing skills are useful
  • Quality and safety are important areas
  • Career: Food industry, quality, production and research

🐄 Animal Husbandry & Livestock

Agriculture-allied education can include livestock production, animal management, nutrition and farm systems.

  • Programme names vary by institution
  • Biological science knowledge is useful
  • Can connect with livestock and farm management
  • Career: Livestock sector, agribusiness and related fields

💼 Agribusiness Management

Combines agriculture with business, marketing, supply chains, finance and rural enterprise.

  • Available as UG or PG pathways depending on institution
  • Useful for students interested in agriculture and business
  • Management and communication skills are valuable
  • Career: Agribusiness, supply chain, sales and management

🌍 Agricultural Economics

Applies economics to farming, agricultural markets, rural development, resources and food systems.

  • Economics and agriculture-oriented pathway
  • Quantitative and analytical skills are useful
  • Higher study can lead to specialised research
  • Career: Policy, research, agribusiness and economic analysis

Popular Agriculture Specialisations

🌾 Agronomy

Crop production, soil management, farming systems and sustainable agricultural practices.

🌱 Soil Science

Soil properties, fertility, nutrients, conservation and sustainable soil management.

🐛 Entomology

Study of insects and their relationship with crops, ecosystems and agricultural production.

🦠 Plant Pathology

Study of plant diseases, pathogens, diagnosis and crop protection.

🧬 Genetics & Plant Breeding

Plant genetics, breeding methods and development of improved crop varieties.

💧 Irrigation & Water Management

Water use, irrigation systems, resource efficiency and agricultural water management.

📡 Precision Agriculture

Uses sensors, mapping, data and technology to improve agricultural decision-making and resource use.

♻️ Sustainable Agriculture

Focuses on resource conservation, environmental sustainability and resilient agricultural systems.

Eligibility & Admission

After Class 12

Students can explore undergraduate agriculture and allied programmes after Class 12, subject to the course's current subject and eligibility requirements.

Science Subjects

Many programmes use combinations of Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics or Agriculture-related subjects. Requirements vary by course and institution.

Minimum Marks

Minimum qualifying marks and other conditions vary by university, programme and admission pathway.

Check Official Rules

Entrance tests, counselling procedures, subject combinations and eligibility can change. Always check the current official admission notification before applying.

CUET-UG

Universities and states may conduct their own entrance examinations or counselling processes for selected programmes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is B.Sc Agriculture?

B.Sc Agriculture is an undergraduate agricultural science programme covering areas such as crop production, soil, plant science, agricultural economics and related subjects.

Is Agriculture only about farming?

No. Agriculture education also covers research, technology, food systems, agribusiness, horticulture, forestry, fisheries, soil, environment and other allied areas.

Can Science students study Agriculture?

Eligible Science students can apply to many agriculture programmes, subject to the current requirements of the institution and course.

Can Agriculture graduates pursue higher studies?

Yes. Relevant postgraduate, research and specialised agriculture and allied pathways are available.

Can Agriculture graduates work in companies?

Yes. Opportunities may exist in agribusiness, food, agricultural inputs, technology, supply chains, research and related industries.

Is Agricultural Engineering the same as B.Sc Agriculture?

No. B.Sc Agriculture focuses primarily on agricultural science, while Agricultural Engineering applies engineering principles to agriculture and related technology.
