Design & Architecture Courses

Explore Architecture, Design and Creative Technology courses in India, including B.Arch, B.Des, Interior Design, Fashion Design, Product Design, Graphic Design, M.Arch, M.Des and related career pathways.

Explore your pathway: Architecture focuses on buildings, spaces and the built environment, while Design covers visual communication, products, fashion, interiors, user experience and other creative problem-solving fields.

Design & Architecture: An Overview

These fields combine creativity, technical knowledge, visual thinking, problem-solving and project work. Students should consider the type of work they enjoy, portfolio requirements and the specific admission rules of each institution.

🏛️ Architecture

Building design, planning, structures, construction, urban spaces and the built environment.

🎨 Design

Visual communication, products, interfaces, fashion, interiors and user-centred creative solutions.

💻 Digital Design

UI/UX, interaction design, graphics, animation and digital experiences combine creativity with technology.

Architecture Courses

🏛️ B.Arch

Bachelor of Architecture is a professional undergraduate programme focused on architectural design, planning, building technology and the built environment.

  • Professional undergraduate architecture pathway
  • Usually a 5-year programme
  • Eligibility depends on current regulatory and institution requirements
  • Career: Architecture, design, planning and related fields

🏙️ B.Plan

Bachelor of Planning focuses on planning of cities, regions, infrastructure and human settlements.

  • Undergraduate planning programme
  • Focuses on urban and regional development
  • Combines planning, analysis and spatial studies
  • Career: Planning, urban development and related fields

🏗️ M.Arch

Master of Architecture provides advanced study and specialisation in architecture and related disciplines.

  • Postgraduate architecture pathway
  • Requires a relevant qualifying degree as per institution rules
  • Specialisations vary by university
  • Career: Advanced practice, design, research and academics

🌆 Urban & Regional Planning

Planning-oriented study covers cities, communities, infrastructure, land use and sustainable development.

  • UG and PG pathways are available
  • Spatial and analytical skills are useful
  • Can connect architecture, geography and public planning
  • Career: Urban planning, development and research

Popular Design Courses

🎨 B.Des

Bachelor of Design is an undergraduate programme covering design thinking, visual communication, products, interfaces and other design disciplines.

  • Usually a 4-year undergraduate pathway
  • Specialisations vary by institution
  • Studio and project work are important
  • Career: Product, communication, UI/UX, fashion and other design fields

🛋️ Interior Design

Interior design focuses on planning and designing functional, aesthetic and user-friendly interior spaces.

  • UG, diploma and certificate pathways available
  • Spatial planning and visualisation are important
  • Software and portfolio skills are useful
  • Career: Interior design, space planning and related services

👗 Fashion Design

Fashion design covers apparel, textiles, styling, garment development and fashion communication.

  • UG and PG pathways available
  • Creative and practical skills are important
  • Portfolio and project work may be required
  • Career: Fashion, apparel, styling, merchandising and design

📦 Product / Industrial Design

Product design combines user needs, creativity, materials, form, function and manufacturing considerations.

  • Design and problem-solving focused
  • Sketching, modelling and digital tools are useful
  • Strong project portfolio can be valuable
  • Career: Product, industrial and design development roles

🖥️ Graphic / Communication Design

Focuses on visual communication through typography, branding, graphics, publications and digital media.

  • Creative and communication-focused
  • Design software skills are useful
  • Portfolio is important for many design pathways
  • Career: Graphic design, branding, visual communication and media

📱 UI/UX & Interaction Design

Focuses on designing digital interfaces and user experiences for websites, apps and digital products.

  • Combines design, technology and user research
  • Wireframing and prototyping are useful skills
  • Portfolio-based projects are valuable
  • Career: UI, UX, product and interaction design

🎬 Animation & Visual Effects

Creative technology programmes can cover animation, visual storytelling, motion graphics and digital production.

  • UG and specialised pathways available
  • Drawing, storytelling and digital tools may be important
  • Portfolio and project work are valuable
  • Career: Animation, motion graphics, VFX and digital media

💎 Jewellery & Lifestyle Design

Specialised design programmes can focus on jewellery, lifestyle products, materials, craft and related creative industries.

  • Specialised design pathway
  • Design development and material knowledge are useful
  • Availability varies by institution
  • Career: Jewellery, lifestyle and product design

Eligibility & Admission

After Class 12

Students can explore B.Arch, B.Des, B.Plan and other undergraduate design pathways, subject to the programme's current eligibility requirements.

Architecture Requirements

Architecture admission can have specific subject, marks and aptitude or entrance requirements. Check the latest applicable rules before applying.

Design Eligibility

Design programmes may accept students from different academic streams. Subject and minimum-mark requirements vary by institute.

Portfolio

Some design institutions may require a portfolio, studio test, interview or other assessment. Requirements vary by programme.

Design & Architecture Entrance Exams

NATA

The National Aptitude Test in Architecture is used by participating architecture institutions according to their current admission rules.

JEE Main Paper 2

The architecture and planning papers of JEE Main may be relevant for participating institutions and programmes according to current admission rules.

NID DAT

The Design Aptitude Test is associated with admissions to specified design programmes at the concerned institution.

NIFT Entrance

NIFT conducts its admission process for specified fashion and design programmes according to its current rules.

UCEED

UCEED is used for undergraduate design admissions at participating institutions according to their admission policies.

Institute-Specific Tests

Many universities and design schools may conduct their own entrance tests, studio assessments, portfolios or interviews.

Building a Design Portfolio

Start Early

Keep sketches, design exercises, photography, models, illustrations and other relevant projects organised.

Show Your Process

A strong portfolio can show how you understand a problem, explore ideas, develop concepts and reach a final solution.

Use Digital Tools

Depending on your field, include suitable digital work such as layouts, prototypes, 3D models or interface projects.

Keep It Relevant

Follow the exact portfolio format and submission instructions provided by the institution you are applying to.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between B.Arch and B.Des?

B.Arch is focused on architecture and the built environment, while B.Des covers broader design disciplines such as products, communication, digital experiences and other areas.

Can I study design after Class 12?

Yes. Many undergraduate design programmes accept students after Class 12, subject to their specific eligibility and admission requirements.

Is drawing compulsory for every design course?

Drawing and visual thinking can be useful, but requirements vary significantly by discipline and institution. Check the current entrance and curriculum requirements.

Is a portfolio required for design admission?

Some institutions require a portfolio or studio assessment, while others use entrance tests and interviews. Requirements differ by institution.

Can Science students study Architecture?

Eligible students from the required academic background can apply to architecture programmes subject to current regulatory and institution-specific requirements.

Can Commerce or Arts students study Design?

Many design programmes accept students from different streams, but the exact eligibility depends on the institution and course.
