Arts & Humanities Courses

Explore undergraduate and postgraduate pathways in Arts and Humanities, including Psychology, English, History, Political Science, Sociology, Economics, Languages, Journalism, Public Administration and more.

Explore your pathway: Arts and Humanities can lead to careers in education, research, media, psychology, public policy, civil services, writing, communication, social research and many other fields.

Arts & Humanities: An Overview

Arts and Humanities help students understand people, society, culture, language, history, ideas and institutions. Many subjects also develop communication, research, analytical and critical-thinking skills that can be useful across careers.

🧠 Psychology & Behaviour

Study human behaviour, cognition, development, personality and social interaction.

📚 Languages & Literature

Explore language, literature, communication, writing, culture and interpretation.

🏛️ Society & Governance

History, political science, sociology, economics and public administration examine society and institutions.

Popular Arts & Humanities Courses

🧠 BA Psychology

Studies human behaviour, cognition, development, personality and psychological processes.

  • Undergraduate psychology pathway
  • Research and analytical skills are useful
  • Higher study is important for many specialised psychology careers
  • Career: Education, research, HR, social services and related fields

📖 BA English

Covers English literature, language, writing, criticism and communication.

  • Undergraduate humanities programme
  • Strong reading and writing skills are valuable
  • Can lead to postgraduate study and specialisation
  • Career: Writing, education, media, publishing and communication

🏺 BA History

Explores historical events, societies, cultures, political developments and historical interpretation.

  • Undergraduate humanities programme
  • Research and critical analysis are important
  • Useful for higher studies and public-service preparation
  • Career: Education, research, museums, heritage and related fields

🏛️ BA Political Science

Studies political institutions, governance, political ideas, public affairs and international relations.

  • Undergraduate social-science pathway
  • Useful for students interested in governance and public affairs
  • Higher studies can lead to specialisation
  • Career: Education, research, policy and public-service pathways

👥 BA Sociology

Examines society, social institutions, communities, inequality, culture and social change.

  • Undergraduate social-science programme
  • Research and fieldwork may be included
  • Useful for social research and higher education
  • Career: Research, social services, education and development sectors

📊 BA Economics

Studies economic systems, markets, policy and quantitative approaches to economic analysis.

  • Undergraduate economics pathway
  • Mathematics and analytical skills can be useful
  • Higher studies can support specialised careers
  • Career: Economics, finance, research, analytics and policy

🌍 Geography

Explores physical and human geography, environments, populations, places and spatial relationships.

  • UG and PG pathways available
  • Fieldwork and spatial analysis may be included
  • Can connect with planning and environmental studies
  • Career: Education, research, planning and geography-related fields

🏢 Public Administration

Studies public organisations, administration, governance, policy implementation and public institutions.

  • UG and PG pathways available
  • Useful for students interested in governance
  • Relevant to public-sector and policy-oriented study
  • Career: Administration, research, policy and related fields

🌐 Foreign Languages

Language programmes develop proficiency, translation, interpretation and cultural understanding.

  • UG, PG, diploma and certificate options may be available
  • Language proficiency is a core skill
  • Useful as a specialised or additional qualification
  • Career: Translation, interpretation, teaching, tourism and communication

📰 Journalism & Mass Communication

Covers reporting, media, communication, digital content, broadcasting and journalism practices.

  • UG and PG pathways available
  • Writing, communication and digital skills are valuable
  • Practical projects can build a portfolio
  • Career: Journalism, digital media, content and communication

Other Arts & Humanities Pathways

Philosophy

Explores ideas, reasoning, ethics, knowledge and philosophical traditions through critical analysis.

Anthropology

Studies human societies, cultures, communities and human development from different perspectives.

Archaeology

Focuses on material remains, heritage, ancient societies and methods of archaeological research.

Library & Information Science

Covers information organisation, libraries, digital resources, information services and knowledge management.

Social Work

Focuses on social welfare, communities, development, counselling-related support and social intervention.

Development Studies

Examines social and economic development, public policy, communities and development challenges.

Postgraduate Arts & Humanities Courses

MA Psychology

Advanced study in psychology with opportunities for specialisation depending on the university.

MA English

Postgraduate study in literature, language, criticism, cultural studies and related areas.

MA History

Advanced historical research and study across selected periods, regions and themes.

MA Political Science

Advanced study of political theory, institutions, governance, international relations and public affairs.

MA Sociology

Advanced social research and study of society, institutions, culture and social change.

MA Economics

Postgraduate economics with advanced analytical and quantitative study.

MA Journalism / Mass Communication

Advanced media, journalism, communication and digital content studies.

MSW – Master of Social Work

Postgraduate professional education in social work, community development and welfare-related practice.

Eligibility & Admission

After Class 12

Students from eligible academic streams can explore BA and other undergraduate Arts and Humanities programmes.

Subject Requirements

Some programmes may have specific subject or minimum-mark requirements. These vary by university and course.

Entrance & Merit

Colleges may use entrance examinations, qualifying marks, interviews or other admission criteria.

After Bachelor's Degree

Graduates can explore MA, specialised postgraduate study, professional programmes and relevant employment.

Arts & Humanities Entrance & Admission Pathways

CUET-UG

Participating universities may use CUET-UG for eligible undergraduate admissions according to their current programme requirements.

CUET-PG

Participating institutions may use CUET-PG for eligible postgraduate admissions according to current rules.

University Entrance

Some universities conduct their own entrance examinations or admission assessments for selected programmes.

Merit-Based Admission

Some institutions admit students based on qualifying examination performance or institution-specific criteria.

Career Scope After Arts & Humanities

🎓 Education

Higher qualifications can support careers in teaching, academic research and educational services.

🧠 Psychology & Behaviour

Psychology graduates can explore research, education, HR and other relevant fields. Many specialised roles require further qualifications.

📰 Media & Communication

Journalism, writing, digital content, publishing and communication offer opportunities for graduates with strong language and media skills.

🏛️ Policy & Governance

Political science, economics, sociology and public administration can support policy and governance-related study and careers.

🔬 Research

Humanities and social-science graduates can pursue research in universities, institutes, surveys, policy and social studies.

🌍 Development & Social Services

Social work and related fields can lead to opportunities in community development, welfare and development organisations.

Skills That Help in Arts & Humanities Careers

🗣️ Communication

Presentation, listening and interpersonal communication are valuable across humanities and social-science careers.

🔎 Research & Analysis

Reading critically, evaluating sources, analysing information and conducting research are important academic skills.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Arts a good option after Class 12?

Arts and Humanities offer many academic and career pathways. The right course depends on your interests, strengths and intended career direction.

Can Arts students study Psychology?

Many psychology programmes accept eligible students from different academic backgrounds, but requirements vary by institution.

Can I study Economics in Arts?

Yes. Economics is offered through many undergraduate and postgraduate pathways. Mathematics requirements vary by programme.

Can Arts students prepare for government exams?

Yes. Arts and Humanities subjects can provide useful foundations for several competitive examinations, subject to each examination's eligibility and syllabus.

What can I do after BA English?

Options include higher studies, teaching, writing, publishing, media, communication, content and other careers depending on skills and additional qualifications.

Does Psychology require higher studies?

Many specialised psychology roles require postgraduate or other professional qualifications. Requirements depend on the role and applicable regulations.
