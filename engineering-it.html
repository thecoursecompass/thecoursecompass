Frequently Asked Questions

Which engineering course has the best future?

Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, Data Science and Robotics are among the fastest-growing fields.

Can Commerce students study BCA?

Yes. Many universities allow Commerce students to pursue BCA.

Is BCA better than B.Tech?

B.Tech focuses on engineering fundamentals, while BCA focuses on software development and computer applications.

Which engineering has more government jobs?

Civil, Mechanical, Electrical and Electronics Engineering offer many PSU and government opportunities.

What is the duration of B.Tech?

B.Tech is generally a four-year undergraduate engineering degree.

Which entrance exams are required for engineering admissions?

JEE Main, JEE Advanced, CUET UG, State CETs and GATE are among the major engineering entrance exams.
