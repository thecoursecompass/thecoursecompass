Medical & Allied Health Sciences

Category structure: This page combines Medical and Allied Health Sciences. Nursing and Pharmacy will be covered separately on The Course Compass.

Medical & Allied Health Sciences: An Overview

Healthcare offers many career pathways. Some professionals provide direct medical care, while allied health professionals support diagnosis, treatment, critical care, rehabilitation and specialised clinical services.

🩺 Medical Courses

Professional programmes such as MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BUMS and BSMS.

🫀 Allied Health

Cardiac Care, Cardiovascular Technology, Perfusion, Radiology, Laboratory, Respiratory Therapy and more.

🏥 Healthcare Careers

Career opportunities exist across hospitals, diagnostic centres, clinics, research, rehabilitation and healthcare organisations.

Medical Courses

MBBS

Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery is the primary undergraduate pathway for students aiming to become doctors.

  • Professional undergraduate medical degree
  • PCB/PCMB background generally required
  • Applicable medical entrance and counselling rules apply
  • Career: Doctor, hospitals, clinics, public health and more

BDS

Bachelor of Dental Surgery prepares students for professional careers in dentistry and oral healthcare.

  • Professional undergraduate dental programme
  • PCB/PCMB background generally required
  • Applicable entrance and counselling rules apply
  • Career: Dentist, hospitals, clinics and academics

BAMS

Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery focuses on Ayurveda-based healthcare and clinical practice.

  • Professional undergraduate programme
  • Science/PCB eligibility generally applies
  • Applicable entrance and counselling rules should be checked
  • Career: Ayurveda healthcare, hospitals, wellness and research

BHMS

Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery is a professional programme in homoeopathic healthcare.

  • Professional undergraduate programme
  • Science/PCB eligibility generally applies
  • Current admission rules should be verified
  • Career: Clinical practice, hospitals, research and teaching

BUMS

Bachelor of Unani Medicine and Surgery provides professional education in Unani medicine.

  • Professional undergraduate programme
  • Science background generally required
  • Applicable entrance and counselling rules vary
  • Career: Unani healthcare, research and academics

BSMS

Bachelor of Siddha Medicine and Surgery provides professional education in Siddha medicine.

  • Professional undergraduate programme
  • Science/PCB eligibility generally applies
  • Current admission requirements should be checked
  • Career: Siddha healthcare, research and teaching

Allied Health Sciences Courses

Allied health professionals play important roles in diagnosis, treatment support, critical care, technology-based healthcare and rehabilitation.

🫀 Cardiac Care Technology

Focuses on cardiac investigations, monitoring and supporting cardiac-care teams in clinical settings.

❤️ Cardiovascular Technology

Covers cardiovascular diagnostics, procedures, monitoring and specialised cardiac healthcare services.

🫁 Perfusion Technology

Specialised field associated with cardiopulmonary bypass and extracorporeal circulation support during relevant procedures.

🔬 Medical Laboratory Technology

Focuses on laboratory testing and diagnostic support across clinical healthcare settings.

☢️ Radiology & Medical Imaging

Covers medical imaging technologies used to support diagnosis and patient care.

🫁 Respiratory Therapy

Focuses on respiratory care, breathing-related conditions, monitoring and clinical support.

🏥 Operation Theatre Technology

Prepares students for technical and clinical support roles in operating theatre environments.

💉 Anaesthesia Technology

Covers equipment, monitoring and technical support associated with anaesthesia and perioperative care.

🩸 Dialysis Technology

Focuses on dialysis equipment, procedures and technical support for renal-care services.

🚑 Emergency & Trauma Care

Focuses on emergency healthcare support, trauma-care settings and rapid clinical response environments.

👁️ Optometry

Focuses on vision care, eye examinations, optical services and related healthcare practice.

🏃 Physiotherapy

Focuses on movement, rehabilitation, physical function and recovery from injury or illness.

Eligibility & Admission

Class 12 Background

Many medical and allied health programmes require a science background. Exact subjects depend on the programme.

PCB / PCMB

Biology-based courses commonly require Physics, Chemistry and Biology/Biotechnology, subject to the current rules.

Entrance Requirements

Entrance examinations and counselling requirements vary by course, institution and state.

Always Check Current Rules

Eligibility, entrance exams, age requirements and admission processes can change. Verify the latest official notification before applying.

Entrance Exams & Admission Pathways

NEET-UG

Used for applicable undergraduate medical admissions and certain other programmes according to current regulations.

State Counselling

State counselling systems may allocate seats according to applicable eligibility, domicile and category rules.

Institution / University Admission

Some allied health programmes may have institution- or university-specific admission procedures.

Current Notifications

Always check the relevant official examination authority, university or college for the current admission cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Medical and Allied Health?

Medical programmes prepare students for professional medical practice, while allied health programmes support diagnosis, treatment, monitoring, technology-based care and other specialised healthcare functions.

Where does Perfusion Technology come under?

Perfusion Technology is covered under Allied Health Sciences on The Course Compass.

Where are Cardiac Care courses covered?

Cardiac Care Technology and Cardiovascular Technology are covered under the Allied Health Sciences section of this page.

Are Nursing and Pharmacy included here?

No. Nursing and Pharmacy will have their own separate category pages.

Is NEET required for every allied health course?

No. Admission requirements vary by programme and institution. Students should check the current official admission rules.

Does The Course Compass guarantee admission?

No. We provide course and admission guidance. Final admission decisions are made by the respective institutions according to their rules.
